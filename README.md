# KALI3977

### ZERO TO ADVANCED. LEARN. PRACTISE. COMPETE. MASTER.

Welcome to **KALI3977** — a place to learn cybersecurity, gain practical experience, challenge yourself and grow as a cybersecurity enthusiast.

Whether you're just starting out or already have experience, KALI3977 aims to give you the resources and opportunities to improve your skills.

## What You Can Do Here

- **Learn Cybersecurity:** Explore courses and learning paths, from basic computer concepts to advanced security topics.
- **Practise Your Skills:** Work through hands-on cyber labs and practical exercises.
- **Solve CTF Challenges:** Test your knowledge with challenges across different cybersecurity domains.
- **Play Cyber Games:** Compete with others in exciting multiplayer cybersecurity games.
- **Join Competitions:** Participate in tournaments, campus events and team competitions.
- **Build Your Team:** Connect with other learners, create teams and compete together.
- **Explore Resources:** Discover useful books, technical materials and learning resources.
- **Grow Your Career:** Explore cybersecurity career paths, projects and interview preparation.
- **AI Cyber Mentor:** Get AI-powered learning assistance, subject to usage limits.
